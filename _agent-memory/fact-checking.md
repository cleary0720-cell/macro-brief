# Fact-Checking Rules — ALL agents, every run
Source: Sep 1, 2026 audit of published articles; applied Sep 23, 2026.
Maintained by hand. Agents: read this file, do NOT rewrite or delete it.

## FOMC meeting dates
- FOMC meetings end on a WEDNESDAY; the decision is announced at 2pm ET on the final day.
- Verify every meeting date via WebSearch "FOMC meeting dates 2026 schedule" before writing it.
- September 2026 was **September 15–16** (decision Sep 16). Every July/August article originally said 16–17.
- Run `date` / `cal` to confirm the weekday of any date you write.

## BLS release days
- Employment Situation (jobs report): FRIDAY, 8:30am ET.
- Initial Jobless Claims: THURSDAY, 8:30am ET.
- (The July 2026 jobs article called the Friday Aug 7 release "Thursday.")

## Historical superlatives ("first in years", "lowest since X", "N-year high")
- Check at least 12 months of the series, including revisions, before writing any superlative.
- Payrolls: February 2026 was -92,000, so July 2026's -23,000 was the "first loss since February 2026", not "first in years".
- Core CPI: July 2026's 2.5% tied Jan/Feb 2026. It was the year's low, not "lowest since late 2024".
- Use the exact date instead of rounding: ISM production 58.5 (July 2026) was "highest since November 2021", not "five-year high".

## Recession language
- "Two consecutive negative quarters" is a rule of thumb. Write: "a commonly used rule of thumb; the NBER uses a broader set of monthly indicators."
- Recession probability: cite a model (WebSearch "New York Fed recession probability"). Never estimate it from intuition. (The site once showed 5% while the NY Fed model was ~25%.)

## Mortgage rates
- Never derive them from the 10Y yield. WebSearch "average 30-year mortgage rate" for the Freddie Mac PMMS figure.
- The normal mortgage-to-10Y spread is ~170–200 bps. (The site once printed 7.1–7.4% when PMMS was ~6.67%.)

## Quoting the Fed
- Use the exact qualifier from FOMC statements and minutes. "Several" and "many" mean different things; never upgrade one to the other for effect.
